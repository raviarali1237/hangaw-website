Delete your Hangaw account

This page explains how to delete your Hangaw account, what is removed, and what is kept.

Hangaw is a calorie and nutrition tracking app published by Raviar Ali (package com.hangaw.mobile). You can delete your account and all of the data attached to it at any time, from inside the app or by writing to us.

Option 1 — delete it in the app

  1. Open Hangaw and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Account details.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Choose the reason you are leaving, then tick I understand this cannot be undone.
  5. Tap Permanently delete my account and confirm.

Deletion runs immediately. You are signed out as soon as it finishes, and there is no waiting period or grace period during which the account could be recovered.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email hangaw.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Everything below is permanently erased. None of it can be restored afterwards, by you or by us.

  • Your profile: name, email address, age, height, weight, and your calorie and nutrition targets
  • Every meal you have logged, and every food you created yourself
  • Your saved and favourite foods
  • Your weight entries and water intake
  • Your daily history, streaks and app visit record
  • Your scan usage counters
  • Your subscription record, and any food problem reports you sent us
  • Your sign-in account itself

What is kept, and for how long

Two things are kept, and neither can be traced back to you:

  • Premium payments made on our website, if you made any — only the plan, the amount, the payment method and the transfer reference. Your name, phone number, receipt image and account are removed from it. The record stays so that the same bank transfer cannot be claimed twice, and it is kept for as long as we need to keep payment records.
  • The reason you gave for leaving, if you chose one — together with the language you used the app in, your platform, the app version, and how many whole days the account existed. This record contains no name, no email address, no user ID, and nothing else that identifies you, so it cannot be linked back to you or to your deleted account. It is kept indefinitely and used only to count why people leave. Choosing a reason is not required in order to delete your account.

Nothing else is retained. We do not keep a shadow copy, a suspended account, or a backup of your personal data for later use.

Photos never leave your phone

Your profile picture and your scan history are stored on your device only. They are never uploaded to us, so there is nothing of theirs to delete on our side — removing the app removes them with it.

When you scan a meal or a nutrition label, the photo is sent to OpenAI to be read and turned into nutrition figures. Hangaw does not store that photo, and it is not kept against your account.
