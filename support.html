What does Hangaw do?

Hangaw helps you log the food you eat, track your daily calories and macros against a personal target, log your weight over time, and build custom foods or recipes for meals you cook yourself. Your target is calculated from your body stats, activity level, goal, and how fast you want to get there.

How is my daily calorie target calculated?

We calculate your BMR (calories your body burns at rest) using your sex, age, height, and weight, scale it up by your activity level to get your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure), then add or subtract calories based on your goal and target speed. That final number is your daily calorie target.

Is this medical advice?

No. Hangaw gives general estimates based on standard formulas, not personalized medical guidance. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or are under 18, talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before following any calorie or macro target.
