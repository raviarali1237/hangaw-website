Privacy Policy

How Hangaw collects, uses, and protects your information. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Hangaw handles personal information when you use the app. We collect only what is reasonably needed to provide the features you choose to use.

Information we collect

We collect account details and information you provide, including your profile, body measurements, activity level, goals, food and weight logs, recipes, fasting history, allergens and dietary restrictions, meal plans, pantry items and shopping lists, and app preferences. With your permission, Hangaw may access the camera, notifications, or step count. Meal photos, nutrition label photos, and barcodes are processed only when you choose to scan them.

How we use your information

We use your information to create nutrition estimates and goals, save and synchronize your progress, provide Premium features, secure your account, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support requests. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. Hangaw contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

Storage, AI, and third-party services

Supabase provides account authentication and database hosting; our database is hosted in the European Union. Photos you take or choose in Hangaw, including your profile picture and your scan history, stay on your device and are never uploaded to us. When you use AI meal or nutrition label scanning, the selected photo is sent securely to OpenAI for analysis and is not stored by Hangaw; on iPhone, Hangaw asks your permission before the first photo is sent. For an unknown barcode, the barcode number may be sent to Open Food Facts to find product information. These providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to perform the requested service.

Your choices

You can update your information and manage camera, step, and notification permissions through Hangaw or your device settings. You may delete your account from Profile; this removes the account and user-owned records. Photos are kept only on your device and are removed when you sign out or delete the app. Limited information may be retained only when required by law, security needs, or routine backup schedules.

Security and children

We use access controls, encrypted network connections, and other reasonable safeguards designed to protect your information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Hangaw is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will update the version shown here and, when appropriate, provide a notice in the app.
